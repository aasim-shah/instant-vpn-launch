# Pricing & Plans

## Pricing Philosophy

FyreWay offers custom pricing tailored to your specific needs. We believe every VPN product has unique requirements, so we work with you to create a pricing structure that matches your usage patterns and growth trajectory.

## How Pricing Works

### Custom Pricing Model

Unlike fixed-tier pricing, FyreWay provides personalized quotes based on:

1. **Expected User Volume**
   - Starting user count
   - Projected growth over 6-12 months
   - Peak vs average usage patterns

2. **Geographic Coverage**
   - Number of regions needed
   - Specific countries or continents
   - Data transfer requirements per region

3. **Bandwidth Requirements**
   - Expected monthly data transfer
   - Peak bandwidth needs
   - Buffer for growth

4. **Feature Requirements**
   - Protocol preferences (WireGuard, OpenVPN, IKEv2)
   - Auto-scaling needs
   - Advanced features (SSO, custom integrations)

5. **Support Level**
   - Email support
   - Priority support with faster response
   - 24/7 phone support with dedicated account manager

### Pricing Factors

**What Affects Your Price**:
- ✅ Number of active users
- ✅ Total bandwidth consumption
- ✅ Number of server locations
- ✅ Support level chosen
- ✅ Contract length (annual vs monthly)
- ✅ Enterprise features (dedicated servers, SSO, etc.)

**What Doesn't Affect Your Price**:
- ❌ Number of API calls
- ❌ Dashboard usage
- ❌ Number of team members
- ❌ Monitoring and analytics usage
- ❌ Number of deployments

## Cost Optimization Tips

### How to Minimize Costs

1. **Right-Size Your Plan**
   - Start with accurate user projections
   - Don't over-provision initially
   - Scale up as needed

2. **Optimize Server Locations**
   - Only deploy in regions with active users
   - Remove unused locations
   - Use analytics to guide decisions

3. **Leverage Auto-Scaling**
   - Scale down during low-traffic periods
   - Set maximum limits to prevent surprise costs
   - Configure aggressive scale-down rules

4. **Choose Annual Billing**
   - Save 10-20% with annual commitment
   - Predictable costs for budgeting
   - Additional perks often included

5. **Monitor Usage Actively**
   - Check dashboard regularly
   - Set up usage alerts
   - Identify and fix bandwidth waste

6. **Efficient Protocol Selection**
   - WireGuard uses less bandwidth than OpenVPN
   - Can reduce data transfer costs
   - Better performance = happier users

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a free tier?

We offer free trials on a case-by-case basis, but no permanent free tier. Contact sales to discuss trial options.

### What happens if I exceed my limits?

You'll receive alerts before hitting limits. Overage charges apply for bandwidth. For user limits, we'll work with you to upgrade your plan.

### Can I change my plan mid-month?

Yes, upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades typically take effect at next billing cycle.

### Are there setup fees?

No setup fees for standard plans. Custom integrations or migrations may have one-time fees discussed upfront.

### How is bandwidth calculated?

Bandwidth is total data transferred through your VPN servers (inbound + outbound). Measured monthly and resets each billing cycle.

### What if my usage is very spiky?

We can structure pricing with burst capacity or recommend auto-scaling configurations to handle spikes cost-effectively.

### Do you offer non-profit discounts?

Yes, we offer special pricing for verified non-profit organizations. Contact sales with your non-profit status documentation.

### Can I pay annually?

Yes, annual billing typically includes 10-20% discount. Contact sales for annual pricing quotes.
